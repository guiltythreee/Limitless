Problem 4.1

Drawing two balls from an urn

Two balls are chosen randomly from an urn containing 8 white, 4 black, and 2 orange balls. We win $2 for each black ball selected and lose $1 for each white ball selected. Let X denote our winnings. What are the possible values of X, and what are the probabilities associated with each value?
