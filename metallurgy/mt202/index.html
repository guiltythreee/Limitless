Atomic Structure and Interatomic Bonding

… questions · lesson + quiz

Know why atoms bond the way they do — every macroscopic property in this course traces back to the shape of the interatomic energy curve.

Branches of metallurgy

Physical metallurgy — how the physical/mechanical properties of metals and alloys are controlled by composition, mechanical treatment and heat treatment; the microstructure–property correlation. Extractive (process) metallurgy — obtaining metals from ores. Mechanical metallurgy — behaviour under applied loads. [P1 trap: ore extraction is never physical metallurgy.]

Atomic number, mass and shells

Z = number of protons (defines the element). A = protons + neutrons (mass number). Maximum electrons in shell n: N = 2n² → 2, 8, 18, 32 for n = 1…4. Four quantum numbers: n (shell energy), l (subshell shape: l = 0, 1, 2, 3 → s, p, d, f), mₗ (orbital orientation), mₛ (spin ±½). Pauli exclusion: max two electrons of opposite spin per quantum state. The famous exception: 4s fills before 3d — this single fact is why the transition metals (Fe, Ti, Cr, Mn) exist as a distinct family with variable valence.

Metallicshared delocalised electron gas70–850conducting, ductile, lustrous

Metallic bonding is non-directional — ion cores can slide past one another without breaking a specific bond. That is the origin of ductility, not any 'heat treatment' argument. [P3: bonding energy is potential energy, never thermal.]

The interatomic energy curve E(r)

E(r) = −A/rᵐ + B/rⁿ with n > m so the repulsive wall rises more steeply as r → 0. At r₀ (equilibrium spacing): net force zero, i.e. dE/dr = 0 — but the attractive and repulsive forces are each finite, only their sum vanishes. Three links to engineering properties:

  • Curvature at r₀ → elastic modulus (stiff well = stiff material)
  • Asymmetry of the well → thermal expansion (added energy makes the atom oscillate about a larger average spacing)
  • Depth E₀ → bond energy → melting point

r₀ fixes the atomic diameter — it does not fix coordination number. [P1/P3 favourite.]

Memorise cold

  • l = 0, 1, 2, 3 → s, p, d, f (memorise cold — recurs all course).
  • 4s before 3d → transition metals, variable valence.
