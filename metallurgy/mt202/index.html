Know why atoms bond the way they do — every macroscopic property in this course traces back to the shape of the interatomic energy curve.

Branches of metallurgy

Physical metallurgy — how the physical/mechanical properties of metals and alloys are controlled by composition, mechanical treatment and heat treatment; the microstructure–property correlation. Extractive (process) metallurgy — obtaining metals from ores. Mechanical metallurgy — behaviour under applied loads.

Exam trap (P1)

Ore extraction is never physical metallurgy — that is extractive metallurgy.

Atomic number, mass and shells

Z = number of protons (defines the element). A = protons + neutrons (mass number).

Maximum electrons in shell n:

Nmax⁡=2n2N_{\max} = 2n^2Nmax​=2n2

→ 2, 8, 18, 32 for n = 1…4.

Four quantum numbers:

  • n — shell energy level
  • l — subshell shape (l = 0, 1, 2, 3 → s, p, d, f)
  • mₗ — orbital orientation
  • mₛ — spin (±½)

Pauli exclusion: a quantum state holds at most two electrons, of opposite spin. The famous exception: 4s fills before 3d — this single fact is why the transition metals (Fe, Ti, Cr, Mn) exist as a distinct family with variable valence.

Metallic bonding is non-directional — ion cores can slide past one another without breaking a specific bond. That is the origin of ductility, not any 'heat treatment' argument.

Exam trap (P3)

Bonding energy is potential energy, never thermal.

The interatomic energy curve E(r)

The interatomic energy balances a long-range attraction against a steep short-range repulsion:

E(r)=−Arm+Brn,n>mE(r) = -\frac{A}{r^{m}} + \frac{B}{r^{n}}, \qquad n > mE(r)=−rmA​+rnB​,n>m

(the repulsive wall rises more steeply as r → 0 precisely because n > m).

At r₀ (equilibrium spacing): net force zero, i.e. dE/dr = 0 — but the attractive and repulsive forces are each finite, only their sum vanishes.

Three links to engineering properties:

  • Curvature at r₀ → elastic modulus (stiff well = stiff material)
  • Asymmetry of the well → thermal expansion (added energy makes the atom oscillate about a larger average spacing)
  • Depth E₀ → bond energy → melting point
Key takeaway

r₀ fixes the atomic diameter — it does not fix coordination number.

Exam trap (P1/P3)

The three links above are the course's favourite P1/P3 source: depth of the well gives the melting point; nothing here fixes the coordination number.
