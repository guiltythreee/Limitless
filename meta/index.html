Shared Reward Standard

The policy is centrally defined in the repository; modules supply completion evidence and difficulty, but do not set their own award amounts.

Modifiers and repeat rules

Difficulty multipliers: Foundational ×1.00 · Intermediate ×1.25 · Advanced ×1.50.

Quality bonus: up to 20% of base EXP based on scored evidence. A fully completed assessment passing the provisional 70% threshold can grant +15 EXP once per checkpoint. The first three completed repetitions of the same item in a local day can earn EXP; later repetitions still count as practice but earn 0 EXP. Speed alone never adds EXP.

Level curve: 100 EXP to reach level 2, then each next level costs 25 more EXP than the previous level-up. These calibration values are versioned and intentionally provisional.
