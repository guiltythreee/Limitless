Modifiers and repeat rules

Difficulty multipliers: Foundational ×1.00 · Intermediate ×1.25 · Advanced ×1.50.

Quality bonus: from 0 at 80% quality to a maximum 20% of base EXP at 100%. A fully completed assessment passing the provisional 70% threshold can grant a separate +15 EXP ledger record once per checkpoint. The first three completed repetitions of the same item in a local day can earn EXP; later repetitions still count as practice but earn 0 EXP. Speed alone never adds EXP.

Level curve: 100 EXP to reach level 2, then each next level costs 25 more EXP than the previous level-up. These calibration values are versioned and intentionally provisional.
