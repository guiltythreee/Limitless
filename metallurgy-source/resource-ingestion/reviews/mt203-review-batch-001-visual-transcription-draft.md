## Page 36 — molecular-weight equations

The page shows:

**Number-average molecular weight**

\[
M_n = \frac{\sum_i n_i M_i}{\sum_i n_i}
= \frac{\sum_i w_i}{\sum_i (w_i/M_i)}
\]

**Weight-average molecular weight**

\[
M_w = \frac{\sum_i n_i M_i^2}{\sum_i n_i M_i}
= \frac{\sum_i w_i M_i}{\sum_i w_i}
\]

**z-average molecular weight**

\[
M_z = \frac{\sum_i n_i M_i^3}{\sum_i n_i M_i^2}
= \frac{\sum_i w_i M_i^2}{\sum_i w_i M_i}
\]

The slide defines:
- `n`: “Moles of molecules (n₁ + n₂ + n₃ + … nᵢ)”, described on the slide as weight (`w`) divided by molecular weight (`M`).
- `w`: weight of individual molecules (`w₁ + w₂ + w₃ + … wᵢ`).
- `M`: molecular weight of each molecule.
