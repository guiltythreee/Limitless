WEEKLY EXECUTION

Daily training routine

A repeatable two-hour weekday template. Protect the independent attempt and editorial review rather than rushing through more problems.

60–75 min · Problem solvingAttempt two problems around 100–200 rating points above your current peak. Give each 30–40 minutes independently.
20–30 min · UpsolvingRevisit yesterday’s unsolved problems. Read the editorial only after a serious attempt, then close it and re-derive the solution.
20–30 min · Topic studyStudy one focused topic from the current stage using its linked chapter/reference, then solve a related problem.
Weekly · Contest + reviewTake one live or virtual contest and upsolve missed problems within 24 hours. Record why each attempt failed.
