SUPPLEMENTARY PROBLEMS 5.1–5.6
These six problems were supplied separately from the main question-bank PDF and are retained before the textbook's Problems 5.7–5.41.

5.1. Let X have density f(x)=c(1−x²) for −1<x<1 and zero otherwise. (a) Find c. (b) Find the cumulative distribution function of X.

5.2. A system consisting of one original unit plus a spare has lifetime X with density f(x)=Cxe^(−x/2) for x>0 and zero otherwise. Find the probability that the system functions for at least 5 months.

5.3. For each function, determine whether a constant C can make it a probability density: (i) f(x)=C(2x−x³), 0<x<5/2; (ii) f(x)=C(2x−x²), 0<x<5/2. In both cases f(x)=0 otherwise.

5.4. A device lifetime X (hours) has density f(x)=10/x² for x>10 and zero for x≤10. (a) Find P(X>20). (b) Find F(x). (c) For six such devices, find the probability that at least three function for at least 15 hours. State your assumptions.

5.5. Weekly sales X, in thousands of gallons, has density f(x)=5(1−x)^4 for 0<x<1 and zero otherwise. What tank capacity makes the probability of supply being exhausted equal to 0.01?

5.6. Compute E[X] for: (a) f(x)=(1/4)xe^(−x/2), x>0; (b) f(x)=c(1−x²), −1<x<1; (c) f(x)=5/x², x>5. Each density is zero otherwise.
