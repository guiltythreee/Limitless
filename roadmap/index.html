How to read this

  • Automatic checkpoints read Probability lessons, your Competition mastery marks, MT201 to MT203 answers and your Math Trainer runs. They never change those modules' data.
  • Practice coverage is not an exam result. Metallurgy coverage counts questions you answered correctly; it does not prove mastery. Pair it with the exam simulator checkpoint.
  • Your own checkpoints (ratings, applications, semester results) are things only you can report. They are saved in this browser under one small record and follow your other Limitless data if you are signed in.
  • Targets are starting suggestions, not requirements set by any firm. Edit roadmap/data.mjs to match your real baseline, add your semester 4 to 8 courses, and run node roadmap/test-roadmap.mjs to check the edit.
  • No EXP is awarded here. The map shows progress; global EXP and levels stay on the Progress page.
